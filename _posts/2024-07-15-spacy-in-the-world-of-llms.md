# spaCy in the World of LLMs

Past TAP Institute courses have focused on spaCy. Over the last year, however, large language models (LLMs) have radically changed the natural language processing (NLP) landscape. This course teaches students the basics of spaCy within this new world. We will focus on the key aspects of spaCy while also considering its advantages and disadvantages in 2024.
