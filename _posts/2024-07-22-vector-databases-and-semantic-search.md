# Introduction to Vector Databases and Semantic Searching

In this course, you will learn the fundamentals of vector databases, which have become increasingly popular over the last few years. You will learn what they are, how they work, and, most importantly, how to build them. You will build local vector databases and learn to create cloud-based ones. We will also cover the basics of semantic searching, its advantages and disadvantages, and ways to perform semantic search on local and cloud-based vector databases.
