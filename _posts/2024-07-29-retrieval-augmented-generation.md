# Introduction to Retrieval Augmented Generation

LLMs have two major limitations: Their training data is fixed and they have the potential to hallucinate. In this course you will learn how to address these issues with retrieval augmented generation(RAG). RAG systems combine vector databases with large language models. They let the user pose a question which then retrieves the relevant material from a vector database. These two things are combined and given to a large language model to frame a domain-specific and up-to-date response. You will learn these concepts and build your own RAG applications, specifically with Verba.
