About Me

Hi! I'm William Mattingly, Ph.D.. I'm a digital nomad, NLP engineer, YouTuber, and public speaker. I focus on the ethical applications of machine learning in the Smithsonian Institution's Data Science Lab. I work and speak globally, mostly in South Africa, Europe, and the United States.

Awards and Funded Projects

- NLP Engineer for the Melon-funded (2024-2025) project 92 NY. - Harry Frank Guggenheim co-fellow (2021-2022) for The Bitter Aloe Project.
- Twice ACLS-funded fellow (2023-2024 | 2024-2025) for the Personal Writes the Political Project.
- NLP engineer and data scientist for the NEH-funded Placing the Holocaust.
